BE PREPARED

Safety & preparedness

General guidance only. Follow official instructions for your area.

✚
01

Know warning signs

Watch for new ground cracks, tilting trees or poles, unusual slope movement, and sudden changes in stream water flow.

02

During heavy rainfall

Avoid steep cut slopes, unstable hillsides, riverbanks, and roads with fresh debris or cracks. Do not cross active slide paths.

03

Prepare to move

Keep essential medicines, documents, a torch, water, and charged phone ready. Know local evacuation routes.

04

Follow official advice

Use instructions from local authorities and official disaster-management agencies. This website does not issue official warnings.

Official information: NDMA · India Meteorological Department · Sachet alerts
ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Monitor risk. Prepare early. Protect communities.

LandslideGuard NE is an educational decision-support prototype combining rainfall indicators with transparent rules. Weather is not a direct measurement of slope stability; this system is not validated for operational emergency use.
